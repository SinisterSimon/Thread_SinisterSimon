Counting every sale after a message overstated what the messages caused by 1.6–4.7×.

Thread is a test lab for AI sales agents. Simulated shoppers with real objections talk to pluggable agents, every conversation is scored, and a randomized holdout group measures what each agent really adds, then checks that estimate against the simulator's hidden truth.

How it works

  1. 01

    Simulate a store

    Checkouts, abandonments and shoppers who come back on their own, with the truth recorded but hidden.

  2. 02

    Split at random

    A share of abandoned checkouts gets no message. That holdout is how a real store measures lift.

  3. 03

    Let agents talk

    Each agent texts five kinds of shopper: price-sensitive, unsure about fit, worried about delivery, just browsing, frustrated.

  4. 04

    Score and measure

    Rules plus an AI judge rate every chat; the holdout estimate is checked against the true lift.
